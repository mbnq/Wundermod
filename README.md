# Wundermod
Mod for Call to Arms - Gates of Hell: Ostfront

QoL tweaks for the Last Stand gamemode:

- more ammo in boxes/trucks
- more cp (each alive officer is -200CP)
- better radius for ammo/medic/fuel etc
- preconfigured default settings
- modded maps
- more flexible access to artillery, air support, reconnaissance, engineers, and specialized units.
- increased time before wounded soldiers bleed out.
- adjusted cover damage, prone damage, recoil, and zoom recoil.
- reworked veterancy bonuses, including improvements to health, stamina, vehicle handling, cannon performance, reload speed, and medic abilities.
- AI improvements
- additional safety checks to prevent AI script errors and invalid squad orders.
- and more...

This version is MP stable - no desyncs.

Made in favor of playing as defenders. Solo, coop with AI, or coop with other players.
SP & MP stable version.
